Privacy policy

Last updated: January 2026

Volt Mobile collects only what we need to run the store: order details, delivery address, and contact email. We never sell personal data, and we never have. Analytics are aggregate and privacy-preserving; no cross-site tracking profiles.

You can request a copy of your data or its deletion at any time by emailing privacy@voltmobile.example. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

Terms of service

Orders are confirmed by email. Prices include applicable taxes at checkout; duties for international orders are shown before payment. We reserve the right to cancel orders affected by manifest pricing errors — if that happens you’ll get a full refund and a coupon, not a fight.

VoltCare warranty terms are included with every flagship order. Warranty claims are handled by our own service team — no third-party call centers.

Cookie policy

We use strictly necessary cookies for cart and checkout, and optional cookies only for the features you turn on. No advertising trackers on this storefront, ever. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings without losing the ability to shop.
